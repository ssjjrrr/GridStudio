Film imagery remains under the named holders' copyright and licenses, rather than the application's MIT license.

Adjustments in the screenshot: all panels preserve aspect ratio and use the player's proportional crop-to-fill mode. Sintel additionally uses the player's crop feature to remove baked-in top/bottom black bars, retaining 545 rows beginning at row 88 of the 1280×720 source. The screenshot was not color-graded or composited afterward.

Source films are excluded from the source archive and all application packages. This screenshot was captured on Windows and is not evidence of a Linux/macOS runtime check.
