Replacement, debugging, and rebuilding
-------------------------------------
Shared libraries are under _internal on Windows/Linux and directories such as Frameworks inside
.app/Contents on macOS. After making a backup, you can replace libraries with interface-compatible
versions for debugging. Modifying a macOS bundle requires local signing again; the published build
scripts can rebuild it. Preserve the real library files, directory structure, and symbolic links.
GridStudio imposes no extra restriction on modifying third-party libraries or reverse engineering
needed to debug such modifications; each library's license applies. Source and build.ps1 / build.sh /
scripts/build.py can be used to rebuild with compatible libraries. Upstream links identify source
locations; they do not guarantee that every distribution method meets all licensing obligations.
